What is guar gum used for?

Guar gum is used as a thickening, stabilizing, or binding agent in food, pharmaceuticals, textiles, paper, personal care, and industrial applications.

How is guar gum sourced?

Guar gum is derived from the guar bean, a legume crop. The gum is obtained from the bean and processed for use in different applications.

Are your products sustainable?

Guar gum is plant-derived and biodegradable. We are committed to quality and sustainability throughout our work.
